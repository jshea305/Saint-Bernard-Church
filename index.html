Welcome to Our Parish

St. Bernard’s is a stewardship parish dedicated to growing in faith, serving our local community, and sharing the Gospel of Jesus Christ.

Online Giving Join Our Parish

Welcome Message

St. Bernard’s provides parishioners with opportunities to use their gifts of time, talent, and treasure to deepen their relationship with God. We offer a variety of ministries that provide spiritual, emotional, and physical support to parishioners and the surrounding community.


If you are a new parishioner, please download our Parish Registration Form and drop it off at the office or place it in the collection basket on Sunday.

Mission Statement

Since its founding in 1874, the parishioners of St. Bernard Church have worked together to remain faithful to their mission. We strive to live in accordance with the Gospel and are challenged to love God and neighbor by bringing the Gospel to everyone. We encourage response to our baptismal call, full participation in the life of the Church, and promotion of moral and social teachings.
